Your Path to Ownership

A simple, transparent 7-step journey to acquiring premium properties.

Create Your Account

Start by signing up on STACKLY Auctions. Enter your basic details and set up a secure profile to access our premium listings.

1

Complete Verification

Submit your KYC documents for a quick verification process. This ensures a safe and genuine bidding environment for everyone.

2

Explore Properties

Browse through our curated catalog of residential, commercial, and luxury properties. Review detailed verified documents for each listing.

3

Register for Auction

Select the property you wish to acquire and register for its auction by paying the refundable Earnest Money Deposit (EMD).

4

Place Your Bid

Log in on the auction date and participate in our transparent, real-time bidding interface from the comfort of your home.

5

Win the Property

If you are the highest bidder when the timer ends, congratulations! The property is yours. Your EMD will be adjusted against the final price.

6

Complete Documentation

Pay the token advance and balance amount as per the schedule. Our dedicated team will assist you with the final registration and handover.

7

Premium property auctions. Transparent bidding, verified properties, and seamless ownership transition.
