Win Smart

Bidding Strategies for Success

1. Know Your Limit

Always decide your maximum budget before entering the bid room. Factor in the property cost, registration fees, and potential renovation expenses to avoid overbidding.

2. The Late Bid Strategy

Wait until the final minutes of the auction to place your highest bid. This prevents artificial price inflation and keeps your competitors guessing your true budget.

3. Pre-Approval is Key

Ensure your finances or mortgage pre-approvals are fully sorted. Winning an auction is legally binding, and having funds ready is absolutely crucial.
