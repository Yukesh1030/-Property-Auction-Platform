Frequently Asked Questions

Properties are listed with a starting bid. Registered users can place bids during the auction window. The highest bidder at the end of the auction wins the right to purchase the property.

You must create an account, complete the KYC verification process by uploading required identity documents, and submit the refundable Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) for the specific property you wish to bid on.

All listed properties undergo a stringent legal and physical verification process by our expert team. We review title deeds, encumbrance certificates, and conduct physical inspections before listing.

The winning bidder must pay a percentage of the bid amount within a specified time (usually 24-48 hours) as token advance. The remaining balance must be cleared as per the payment schedule to complete the registration.
